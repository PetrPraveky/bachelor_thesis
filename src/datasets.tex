\section{Dataset Mapping}

Four mapping frameworks that support \ac{ROS2} were chosen: LOSOS, OctoMap~\cite{bk_octomap}, WaveMap~\cite{bk_wavemap} and RayFronts~\cite{bk_rayfronts}. The selected mapping frameworks differ in their primary purpose, but they partially overlap in the supported map representations. The overlap is mainly visible in semantic mapping, \ac{ESDF} based representations, and occupancy mapping. On \reftab{tab:supported_mapping_features} is shown which features are supported by which mapping frameworks.

\begin{table*}[!h]
	\centering
	\noindent

		OctoMap & No & No & Yes \\
		WaveMap & No & Yes & No \\
		RayFronts & Yes & No & No
	\end{tabular}
